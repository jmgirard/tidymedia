# M113: One call says which programs tidymedia found, and a remembered location can be forgotten

**Status:** done (2026-09-06, PR #117 https://github.com/jmgirard/tidymedia/pull/117)

**Goal:** Export `program_status()`, reporting the resolved path and reported version of all four programs in one call, and `unset_program()`, removing a remembered location `set_program()` wrote.

**Outcome:** `program_status()` returns a `program`/`location`/`version` tibble over `ffmpeg`, `ffprobe`, `ffplay`, `mediainfo`, built on `tool_versions()` widened from two programs to four by a `programs`/`locations` pair (the manifest's own call unchanged) with `parse_version_line()` gaining MediaInfo's `--version` shape; it suppresses `find_program()`'s per-program warnings and reports `NA` instead. `unset_program(program)` — argument required, no default, per D079 — removes both `<program>_location.txt` files, the `tools::R_user_dir()` one and the pre-M097 `rappdirs` one, through the `tm_unlink()` seam, answers from a third filesystem read rather than `unlink()`'s status, drops the memoized FFmpeg capabilities, warns `tidymedia_no_remembered_location` carrying `tm_program` and returns `FALSE` where nothing was remembered, and aborts `tidymedia_location_not_removed` where a removal does not take. `program_status()` joined the computed timeout domain, `tm_timeout_argumentless()`, the absorber partition and a named `tm_timeout_post_baseline()` exclusion; `tm_force_timeout()` now intercepts at `find_program()` as well as at the two spawn wrappers, with a grid case running the whole domain under an emptied `PATH`, because `program_status()` is the first domain member whose spawning is conditional on the machine.

**Decisions:** milestone-local (2026-09-05): both names as planned over `program_versions()`/`forget_program()`; a three-column tibble with no `found` logical restating `is.na(location)`; a warning and `FALSE` rather than an abort for nothing-remembered; no `confirm` argument. D079 decided `program`'s absent default at the review gate.

**Review:** two full three-lens fan-outs. The first returned ten findings, one of which (F4) falsified AC3 outside the domain of the walk it named and routed to the gated amendment protocol — AC3's clause "`find_program()` returns what it returned before the `set_program()` call" was replaced by "answers as it did when nothing was remembered", after two re-audits and their five findings; the same false promise was fixed in `?unset_program`, its example and NEWS. That pass also found CI red on macOS and Windows, whose runners install no binaries, which produced T7. The re-review's fourteen findings reproduced six of the first ten and added six. Fixed at the gate: the `unset_program()` no-argument deletion (D079), the NEWS "warns about nothing" overclaim, `?program_status`'s incomplete lookup order, a helper comment claiming a recording mock, and a test asserting invisibility but not `TRUE`. Nine went to two candidate rows; F8 stayed rejected. All eight CI legs green at merge.
