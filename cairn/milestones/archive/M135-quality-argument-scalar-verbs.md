# M135: The re-encoding task functions take a `quality` argument that passes the encoder's own rate-control value through

**Status:** done (2026-09-17, PR #139 https://github.com/jmgirard/tidymedia/pull/139)

**Goal:** A caller sets the encoder's own quality number on a re-encoding task function without building a pipeline by hand.

**Outcome:** `quality = NULL` after `fallback` on `anonymize_video()`, `compare_videos()`, `crop_video()`, `format_for_web()`, `picture_in_picture()`, `segment_video()`, `separate_audio_video()` and `standardize_video()`. `quality_flags()` is a seven-row table keyed on the resolved encoder: `libx264`/`libx265` take `-crf` (0 to 51), the three nvenc encoders `-cq` (0 to 51), the two videotoolbox encoders `-q:v` (1 to 100). `check_quality()` refuses a value that is not one finite number, is out of range, or names an encoder outside the table, with the encoder named. `emit_video_codec()` checks it against `intended_encoder()` (pure) above the availability probe and emits through `emit_quality()` -> `ffm_output_options()`, so the flag lands after `-codec:v`. The resolver body moved to `resolve_hw_encoder_info()` (encoder plus `fell_back`). A fallback drops `quality` and the message says so. `segment_video()` checks at its front door above `purrr::pmap()`, and `check_quality_needs_reencode()` refuses `quality` with `reencode = FALSE`. `format_for_web_pipeline()` and `anonymize_pipeline()` now reach the resolver through `emit_video_codec()`. `quality_param()` in `R/task-doc.R` writes the 8 help entries; the vignette section "Using video hardware" and NEWS document it. Tests: `test-quality.R`, `test-quality-seam.R`, and `test-quality-grid.R` over AC1's filter (8 verbs, 52 pairs, refusals against a probe counter, both fallback backends).

**Decisions:** `quality` after `fallback` in every signature. Drop `quality` on fallback with the message saying so, over refusing `fallback = TRUE`. Refuse an encoder the table lacks over passing `-crf` to any software name. One Layer 2 helper over a new engine verb (IP1). AC1's filter expression amended with `ifnotfound = list(NULL)` because `mget()` over the exports hits rlang's reexported active binding.

**Review:** three-lens fan-out, 12 findings, all logged. Fixed now: the NEWS `run` position table was stale by one on four verbs, and its inserted-argument list lacked `quality`. The AC2 grid asserted `min + 1` only, and now asserts `max` too. Rejected with measurement: the positional shift (no 0.1.0 caller had `hardware`, `fallback` or `audio_stream`), scientific-notation values (FFmpeg accepts `-crf 1e-05` and `23.5`), and the pipeline reroute an M106 gate declined (12 compiled commands byte-identical between master and HEAD). The prior-review lens found nothing. CI: 8 workflow checks green. Codecov patch 82% (27 lines, all `quality_param()`) and project 93.71% reported red and were merged past as advisory. One LESSONS line added (the `mget()` trap). At hygiene: the review's `document()` no-diff ran under roxygen2 8.0.0 against the 8.1.0 pin, which is not evidence (LESSONS M128). `man/` and `NAMESPACE` regenerated in a scratch copy with the pin lowered are identical to the merged tree.
